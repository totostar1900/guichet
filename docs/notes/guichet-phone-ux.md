State on 2026-09-19 evening (master `9940e13`, live on Vercel). One day of phone work with the user, decision by decision.

**Gestures (decided by the user).** Swipe = the neighbouring line, on the fiche only: `src/components/mobile/SwipePager.tsx` wraps the fiche (`id` prop → neighbours from the sessionStorage list memory `guichet:listOrder`, which now carries `titles`); the drag follows the finger, the neighbour peeks in with its words stuck under the header (`.peekBody` sticky) and, past 30 % or a flick, says « Relâchez pour ouvrir », then `router.push` with `guichet:swipeDir` for the enter animation; first visit per device: a 38 px step aside + toast (`guichet:hint:swipe:fiche`, written when the toast shows so StrictMode's double effect does not eat it). The page swipe between lists (Titres ⇄ Fonds) was built, then **removed (F2)**: the cards own the horizontal gesture. `src/components/mobile/SwipeActions.tsx`: a card pulled left reveals Suivre (good) · Comparer (navy) · Plus (gold-ink, opens the « ··· » sheet); a long pull past 70 % follows at once; touch only under 760 px. Long press was built then **removed** (redundant with « ··· »). Not done on purpose: pull-to-refresh (native on Android, none on iOS), swipe between the two market switches.

**The card pull, second version (2026-09-19 evening, `feb90dc` … `e0b4815`).** Suivre · Comparer · Plus left the pull (redundant with « ··· »). Pull left = « Déclarer » (fiche form, `#intention`) and « Me rappeler » (`?intent=rappel#intention`), a « ‹ » handle, a long pull fires Déclarer. Pull right = a 3D turn (`rotateY`, a width of pull = half a turn, a quarter turn commits) to a light-grey back (the user chose « dos clair »): `src/components/mobile/CardBack.tsx` = calendar with current step or dated facts (from `src/lib/domain/back.ts`, pure: `backFacts(offer, now)`, `fundBackFacts(row)`), the curve fetched on first turn by the server action `lineCurve(id)` in `src/app/offres/[id]/actions.ts` (60 closes / 60 NAVs / ≥3 past auctions of the same Treasury), and the fiche's reference calculation; compact view adds the list's four figures on top. Both the wrapper and the turning box take the back's height (the back is absolute inside the turning box: sizing only the wrapper clipped it), a ResizeObserver follows the curve's arrival, the CardBack effect must not depend on its own state (it cancelled its own fetch once). Leaving: slide back 40 px from anywhere, tap the back or the handle, tap elsewhere, scroll. Mockups: https://claude.ai/artifact/DFXLdfvUkxWrCLtus4V3Me (what to put under a pull), https://claude.ai/artifact/Kj3X47EcihAMuVeD5DLYwc (the back), https://claude.ai/artifact/PfLS5uYcZc3o3tyg668CvU (C, D, E by instrument).

**The « ··· » (LineMenu).** `src/components/mobile/LineMenu.tsx`: three bold gold dots, nothing around them (the user rejected a bordered chip, then a gold chip); the sheet has a navy head with the title and the figure in gold, gold icon chips, darker scrim; actions: Voir la fiche · Suivre/Ne plus suivre (toggleWatch; not signed in → /connexion) · Comparer avec… · Partager le lien (navigator.share, else copy) · Copier l'ISIN (clipboard, execCommand fallback, then the value as a toast; never `prompt()`, the pane forbids it) · Fiche PDF on the fiche. « Déclarer une intention » is never in the menu. `openRef` lets the card's pull open the same sheet. On cards it sits in the top-right corner under the status; on table rows beside « Voir la fiche »; list rows get it absolutely on the corner (a button cannot live inside the row link).

**Cards (layout D).** `src/components/OfferCard.tsx` and `src/app/fonds/FundCard.tsx` (phone only, `usePhone`; the funds table stays on a desk), both on `OfferCard.module.css`: corner = status + dots, the number, three facts with an optional note under the value (`OfferSummary.facts` is `[label, value, note?]`: the duration under the maturity, the inception date under « depuis l'origine »), foot = closing date + « Voir la fiche → ». No coloured top edge by default. `src/components/Density.tsx`: density (detail | compact, `guichet:cartes`) and distinction (`guichet:cartes:sep`, default `zebra`; none · zebra · half · full · left · tint · shadow · zebrahalf) chosen in the « Affichage des cartes » sheet beside the density icons; lists set `data-sep` on their container; the rules are at the end of OfferCard.module.css (a CSS-module rule needs a local class, so the container gap for « shadow » lives in the browsers' CSS). Compact = title + [status ···], then number · condition · date.

**Lists.** Nothing frozen any more on Titres or Fonds (the user unfroze both): once the toolbar passes under the header, `src/components/FilterFab.tsx` (round icon, bottom-left, portaled to body, count badge) opens the same controls in `src/components/mobile/Sheet.tsx` (bottom sheet on the phone, dialog on a desk; the Titres FilterSheet gained the search and the sort). `src/components/BackToTop.tsx` bottom-right on Titres, Fonds, Guide, parcours: hysteresis (past 0.8 screen, hidden under 0.3), no backdrop blur (both flickered on the phone); both corners are 36 px, white, one thin line, the user found the navy/gold version intrusive. Each list keeps its own memory `guichet:listScroll:<pathname>` {url, y}, written as the reader scrolls (timer throttle, not rAF), ignored once the address moved on; `useListScroll` re-applies the saved position for one second against the router's scroll-to-top. A fixed element inside the pager's `will-change` ancestor sits in the page, not the viewport: keep BackToTop and the FAB outside or portaled.

**Share image.** `src/app/offres/[id]/opengraph-image.tsx` (ImageResponse, Manrope fetched from Google Fonts at run time with a plain fallback, glyphs outside the font drawn as divs) + `generateMetadata` on the fiche writes og/twitter title and description with the figures. WhatsApp caches previews; add `?v=1` to force a fresh one.

**Browser-pane gotcha.** A hidden pane stalls requestAnimationFrame, so React 19.2's batched Suspense reveal (`$RB`/`$RV`) never runs and a streamed list (OfferBrowser under `<Suspense>`) looks blank with its content in `<div hidden id="S:0">`. Take a screenshot to bring the pane forward, then test. Not a bug.
